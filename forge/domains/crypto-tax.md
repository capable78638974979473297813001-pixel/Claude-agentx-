- Disposals (sales, swaps, spending) are typically taxable events; lot tracking per wallet or universal depends on the rules.
- Income from staking/mining/airdrops is often income at receipt with basis established at that value.
- Transfers between own wallets are not disposals but must be matched to keep basis.
- Rules differ sharply by country and change frequently.
