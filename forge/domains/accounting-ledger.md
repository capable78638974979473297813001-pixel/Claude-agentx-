- Every transaction posts balanced debits and credits; the ledger is append-only with reversing entries for corrections.
- Chart of accounts, periods and period close, accruals vs cash basis.
- Tax liabilities post to liability accounts; tax engines should emit journal entries, not just totals.

Never enter a rate, threshold, or date from memory as verified; cite a primary source or mark it UNVERIFIED.
