- Federal brackets plus provincial/territorial systems (Quebec administers its own return).
- Many credits are nonrefundable at the lowest federal rate applied to a credit base amount.
- Payroll: CPP/QPP contributions and EI premiums with annual maximums.
- Capital gains are partially included in income under inclusion-rate rules; check the current rule for the period.
