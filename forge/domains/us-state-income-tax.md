- States usually start from federal AGI or federal taxable income, with additions and subtractions; conformity to federal law may be rolling, fixed-date, or selective.
- Some states have no broad wage income tax; some use flat rates, others brackets.
- Residents, part-year residents, and nonresidents are treated differently; credits for taxes paid to other states avoid double taxation.
- Some localities levy income taxes, often administered with the state return.
