- No federal sales tax; states, counties, cities, and special districts levy stacked rates.
- Sourcing is origin- or destination-based depending on the state and transaction type.
- Economic nexus rules can require remote sellers to collect.
- Product taxability (food, clothing, digital goods, SaaS) varies widely by state; exemption certificates for resale and exempt organizations.
- Use tax applies when sales tax wasn't collected.
