- Employee federal income tax withholding uses the employer withholding methods publication with the employee's withholding certificate.
- Social Security tax applies up to an annual wage base; Medicare has no wage base and an additional employee-only tax above a threshold.
- Federal and state unemployment taxes are employer-side with their own wage bases and credits.
- Supplemental wages have separate withholding methods.
