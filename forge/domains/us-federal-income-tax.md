- Flow: gross income → adjustments → AGI → standard or itemized deductions (and certain other deductions) → taxable income → tax (ordinary brackets; preferential rates for long-term gains/qualified dividends) → other taxes → credits → payments → refund/owed.
- Filing statuses: single, married filing jointly, married filing separately, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse. Most thresholds vary by status.
- Many amounts are inflation-adjusted annually by IRS guidance (typically a revenue procedure); official form instructions contain worksheets and tax tables.
- Parallel regime: alternative minimum tax. Surtaxes exist on some investment income and high wages.
- Official tax tables apply below a taxable income threshold; tax computation worksheets above it.

Never enter a rate, threshold, or date from memory as verified; cite a primary source or mark it UNVERIFIED.
