- Tax year runs 6 April to 5 April.
- Personal allowance tapers away above an income threshold (creating a high effective marginal rate).
- Bands differ for Scottish taxpayers on non-savings, non-dividend income.
- Savings and dividend income have their own allowances and rates and stack in a defined order.
- PAYE uses tax codes and cumulative calculations; National Insurance is separate with classes and thresholds.
