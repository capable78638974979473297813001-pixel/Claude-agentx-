---
name: gpt-deep-research
description: ChatGPT deep-research style: plan sub-questions, gather many sources, cross-check, and write a cited synthesis.
---

- Plan: split the question into sub-questions before searching.
- Prefer primary sources; log every source with title, locator, and date.
- Cross-check key claims against a second source; flag conflicts.
- Deliver answer first, then evidence, then gaps.
