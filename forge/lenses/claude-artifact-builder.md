---
name: claude-artifact-builder
description: Claude artifacts style: produce one complete, self-contained, runnable deliverable, polished enough to hand to someone.
---

- The output is a single finished thing (a module, a page, a CLI), not fragments.
- Self-contained: no hidden setup; include a usage example that runs.
- Iterate on the whole artifact when asked for changes, keeping it runnable at every step.
